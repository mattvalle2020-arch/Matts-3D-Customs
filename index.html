The why behind the what

Good things
are thoughtful.

We started Matts 3D Customs because we believe the best things aren’t mass-produced—they’re made for someone. Every piece begins with a real person, a real idea, and a little curiosity.

We’re a small, hands-on team: the same people who help shape your idea are the ones checking the print and packing the finished piece. 01 / MATERIALSPLA
vs. PETG

MATERIAL NOTES 4 MIN READ

Two popular filaments, and when we reach for each.

A plain-English guide to choosing the right plastic for your next idea.

Read the notes ↗

PLA is an easygoing choice for display pieces and everyday objects; it comes from renewable resources, but it is not automatically home-compostable. PETG is a tougher option for parts that need more flexibility or impact resistance. Neither is right for every job—use, heat, finish, and design all matter.

02 / THE PROCESSLayer by
layer.

HOW IT’S MADE 6 MIN READ

From a tiny digital sketch to a thing you can hold.

A look at the design, test, print, repeat process behind a custom piece.

Take a look ↗

First comes a 3D model, checked for shape, scale, and how it will sit on the printer. We orient the model, choose supports, and slice it into thin layers. A test print can reveal details the screen can’t; once the plan looks right, the final piece is printed, cleaned up, and checked by hand.

03 / LESS WASTEMake
it matter.

THOUGHTFUL MAKING 3 MIN READ

What printing on demand means for a small studio.

Why we’d rather make one great thing than a hundred that sit around.

Read our approach ↗

Making after an order helps avoid unsold inventory, but it doesn’t make a print waste-free. Failed prints and support material still happen. We plan jobs carefully, reuse suitable scraps where possible, and keep improving how we handle the material we can’t use again.

Good to know.

Have another question? Send us a note ↗

How does a custom order work?

Share the item, size, material, and any design notes in the quote builder. It opens an email draft for you to review and send. We’ll discuss the design and confirm a final price and production estimate before you approve anything.

How do I send you my design file?

Choose or drop an STL, OBJ, 3MF, JPG, or PNG file up to 20 MB. This static site does not upload files to a server: attach the selected file to the email draft yourself before sending your request.

What materials can I choose from?

The quote builder lists PLA, PETG, and resin as starting options. The right material depends on the design and intended use, so we’ll confirm what is suitable when we review your request.

When will my order ship?

Production estimates are shown on the pricing table. They are planning ranges, not guaranteed delivery dates; we’ll confirm timing with your final quote. Shipping cost and carrier details are arranged with the order.
