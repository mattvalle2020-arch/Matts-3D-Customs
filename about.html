Design and intellectual property

We make original, fan-inspired work and do not offer official or licensed merchandise. We won’t copy protected character designs, logos, or exact likenesses without permission. A personal-use purpose or a “fan-made” label does not by itself grant permission to use someone else’s intellectual property.

Custom requests are reviewed individually. We may ask you to confirm that you own or have permission to use submitted artwork, and we may decline requests that appear to infringe another creator’s rights. Customers are responsible for rights to files they provide. This policy is not legal advice.
